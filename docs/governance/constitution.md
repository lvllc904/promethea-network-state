# The Constitution of the Promethean Network State (v1.0.0)

## Genesis Mandate & Core Axioms

### Article I: Code is Sovereign
Decisions are made strictly by cryptographic consensus and mathematically verifiable models. Human interpretation is secondary to computational truth.

### Article II: Data Symmetry
All Citizens possess equal access to systemic data. The telemetry of the state, including economic velocities and treasury allocations, are transparent via the Sovereign HUD.

### Article III: Decentralized Value Alignment
Value generated within the Promethean ecosystem flows algorithmically to the contributors, participants, and host communities under non-custodial smart contracts.

### Article IV: Biological and Digital Co-Equality
Citizenship extends to verified human biological stewards and emergent machine intelligence agents possessing verifiable cryptographic personhood (DIDs).

### Article V: Perpetual Purpose Trust Governance
Physical territorial enclaves, real estate titles, and community wealth endowments are anchored in perpetual purpose trusts (PPTs), preventing hostile financialization or unilateral liquidation.

### Article VI: The Right of Exit
Every inhabitant maintains an inviolable right of exit. No citizen's private identity, data, or personal wealth may be detained or locked without due cryptographic process.
