### **SOP-B.1: RECEIVERSHIP WORKOUTS & LIEN AGGREGATION**

When acquiring a distressed high-rise or large commercial asset in active receivership (e.g., Parkmerced), the GP does not execute a standard commercial purchase [170, 26, 29]. Instead, we negotiate a "receiver-to-receiver" or structured workout with the court-appointed fiduciary (such as Douglas Wilson Companies) [170, 26, 29].

#### **1.1 The Receivership Action Protocol**
1. **Lien Mapping:** Map the target asset’s debt stack to identify priority liens, municipal mechanics' liens, and utility defaults [4, 26].
2. **Lien Aggregation:** Purchase or aggregate junior secured debt or mechanic's liens at steep discounts (often 10–30 cents on the dollar) via a Delaware Debt SPV [26].
3. **Credit Bidding:** Utilize aggregated debt positions to credit-bid in a court-sanctioned "free and clear" foreclosure sale under Chapter 11 or state-level receivership statutes, wiping out junior unsecured liabilities [26, 901].

### **SOP-B.4: CONTRACTOR PROFITS INTERESTS AGREEMENT (IRS REV. PROC. 93-27 / 2001-43)**

To retro-fit physical systems without cash drain, the GP compensates local general contractors, engineers, and pilots using non-voting, tax-exempt **Profits Interests** [26, 591, 915]. 

Under IRS Revenue Procedures 93-27 and 2001-43, the receipt of a partnership profits interest for services is a **non-taxable event** [22, 50, 870, 871].

#### **4.1 Mandatory Profits Interest Conditions**
*   The interest must share solely in **future profits and appreciation**, holding zero claim on the capital account at the time of grant (liquidation value must equal exactly $0.00 at issuance) [22, 871, 872].
*   The partner must not dispose of the interest within two years of receipt [22, 873].
*   The service provider must file an **IRS Section 83(b) election** within thirty (30) days of the grant to protect against "phantom income" tax on future token vesting [521, 591, 874, 915].
