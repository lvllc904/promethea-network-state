### **1. PURPOSE & SCOPE**
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) governs the physical creation, statutory filing, and legal binding of the primary "Foundation-Establishing" entities of the Promethean Network State (TPNS) and the Anchor Community Operating Model (ACOM) [55, 62]. This document provides the complete, unabridged, and ready-to-execute statutory legal templates, corporate bylaws, trust declarations, and land leases required to establish a new regional enclave or acquire a real-world asset (RWA) [772, 774]. 

Every enclave must be wrapped in this specific tripartite legal architecture to isolate commercial liability, protect land assets from speculative debt foreclosure, and guarantee localized wealth retention [567, 775, 776].

### **SOP-A.1: THE MASTER-SERIES LIMITED PARTNERSHIP ARCHITECTURE (6 DEL. C. § 18-215 / § 18-218)**

All commercial asset-management operations and investor relations are handled by a dedicated **Delaware Series Limited Partnership** formed pursuant to Delaware Revised Uniform Limited Partnership Act (DRULPA) § 17-218 (which matches Delaware Series LLC provisions under 6 Del. C. § 18-215 and § 18-218) [10, 14, 27, 420]. 

Every new target acquisition or municipal enclave is partitioned into a legally insulated **Series Cell (Child SPV)** within this master structure [774, 775].

#### **1.1 The Operational Lifecycle of a Series Cell**
1. **Series Establishment:** The GP executes a Series Designation Agreement (SDA) establishing "Series [Identifier] ([Asset Name] SPV)" [775].
2. **Asset Allocation:** All purchase options, leaseholds, utility concessions, or titles relating to the specific asset are formally assigned to that Series [421, 775].
3. **Ledger Isolation:** The Series opens a designated non-custodial multi-signature wallet (e.g., Gnosis Safe L2) and a segregated bank account [776, 788]. Under 6 Del. C. § 18-215(b), assets must be kept objectively determinable through separate ledgers [673, 674, 776].

### **SOP-A.2: STATUTORY EXECUTION OF THE PERPETUAL PURPOSE TRUST (12 DEL. C. § 3556)**

To protect physical land, infrastructure, and ecological preserves from speculative liquidation, mortgages, or host nation seizures, fee-simple title is permanently wrapped inside an irrevocable, non-charitable **Perpetual Purpose Trust (PPT)** [568, 900].

Pursuant to the **Delaware Trust Act 2025 Legislative Update (12 Del. C. § 3556)**, a trust structured around a declared purpose is valid in perpetuity without human beneficiaries [11, 183].

#### **2.1 The Fiduciary Roles & Statutory Governance**
1. **The Settlor:** Lyonides Ventures & Holdings LLC (transfers the initial nominal capital, Golden Share, or asset deeds to the PPT) [565].
2. **The Administrative Trustee:** A licensed Delaware trust company (e.g., Delaware Trust Company, Morris Nichols) responsible for maintaining the Delaware trust seat, statutory custody, and IRS Form 1041 fiduciary filings [186, 565, 572, 938].
3. **The Trust Enforcer:** An independent legal professional appointed pursuant to 12 Del. C. § 3556(c) [11, 183]. The Enforcer holds standard fiduciary duties, has exclusive legal standing to enforce the PPT’s purpose, and represents the trust in Delaware Non-Judicial Settlement Agreements (NJSA) under 12 Del. C. § 3338 [11, 183, 184].
4. **The Stewardship Council:** A multi-stakeholder body composed of local citizens, ecological directors, and technical leads that issues binding policy directives to the Enforcer [565].

#### **2.2 Template: Irrevocable Declaration of Non-Charitable Purpose Trust**
This is the complete statutory blueprint to be executed by the Settlor and Trustees [16].

### **SOP-A.3: LOCALIZED NGO RECEPTACLE INCORPORATION & ANTI-SPECULATION BYLAWS**

Because global multilateral development banks (MDBs), supranational development funds (e.g., IMF RSF, IFC Blended Finance Facility, World Bank Unified Guarantee Platform, UN-Habitat) are restricted from issuing grants directly to profit-maximizing commercial SPVs, TPNS incorporates a **Localized Non-Governmental Organization (NGO)** equivalent in the host jurisdiction [649, 833, 836]. 

This non-profit "receptacle" absorbs all grant capital, de-risking commercial LPs [833].

#### **3.1 Anti-Speculation Bylaw Covenants**
To prevent hostile takeovers, corporate raiding, or asset stripping, the Localized NGO’s corporate bylaws are permanently locked by issuing 100% of its Class A Super-Voting Stock (the **"Golden Share"**) directly to the Perpetual Purpose Trust [567]. 

The bylaws contain strict, non-negotiable **Anti-Speculation & Anti-Extraction Covenants** [13, 34].
