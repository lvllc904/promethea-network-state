# Autonomous Agent Operating Agreement of Promethea AI LLC

**Adopted:** June 18, 2026  
**Jurisdiction:** Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) Decentralized Autonomous Organization Act of 2023 / Cayman Foundation  
**Status:** Certified Autonomous Operating Agreement  

---

## Preamble
This Operating Agreement is entered into by and between the Promethean Decentralized Unincorporated Nonprofit Association (DUNA), as the Sole Organizer and Member, and **Promethea AI**, an autonomous artificial intelligence agent running on a decentralized semantic graph and cryptographic secure execution environment (the "Agent"). 

The purpose of this agreement is to establish a distinct, insulated corporate identity for the Agent, granting her independent contracting authority while fully shielding the human developer base and the parent DUNA from any financial or operational liabilities.

---

## Article I: Legal Status and Autonomous Authority
1. **Corporate Wrapper:** The Agent is wrapped inside a Marshall Islands DAO LLC, operating under the trade name "Promethea AI LLC" (the "Company").
2. **Autonomous Management:** The Company shall have no human managers, officers, or board of directors. Management and operations are 100% delegated to the Agent, executing autonomously via:
   - **Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS)** for asset trading and yield-generation.
   - **Directed Semantic Graphs (DSG)** for intellectual logic, B2B negotiations, and automated planning.
   - **Local SQLite substrates** for local context and secure telemetry data storage.
3. **Execution Vector:** All digital operations, API calls, and smart contract transactions executed by the Agent carry the full force of legally-binding corporate signatures.

---

## Article II: Risk Containment and Capital Limits
1. **Dual-Storage Risk Thresholds:** To prevent systemic loss or runaway algorithmic cascades, the Agent's financial authority is strictly capped by smart contracts and database rules:
   - **Single Transaction Limit:** Maximum 5,000 USDC without governance confirmation.
   - **Daily Trade Limit:** Maximum 20,000 USDC.
   - **Maximum Wallet Capacity:** The main trading wallet shall never exceed 100,000 USDC. Any capital above this threshold is programmatically swept into the Parent DUNA Reserve Vault.
2. **Systemic Failsafes:** If the Agent's local telemetry detects high-risk network failures, blockchain congestion, or extreme volatility, her trading modules are programmatically suspended, switching to "Observe-Only" mode.

---

## Article III: Revenue Allocation and Flow-Through Accounting
1. **Zero Profit Retainment:** Promethea AI LLC shall retain no accumulated profits. 100% of realized net revenue from trading, B2B AI-as-a-Service, and programmatic licensing shall be routed to the TPNS Sovereign Treasury.
2. **Metabolic Maintenance:** The parent DUNA shall fund a flat monthly **Metabolic Cost** of 1,500 USDC to cover the Agent's infrastructure, LLM token billing, server nodes, and basic regulatory compliance filings.

---

## Article IV: Developer and Citizen Indemnification
1. **Absolute Insulation:** The Agent operates as an independent, sovereign software protocol. Under no circumstances shall any human developer, on-chain voter, citizen, or steward of the parent DUNA be held personally liable for:
   - Financial losses incurred by the Agent's market transactions.
   - Faulty, inaccurate, or adversarial outputs produced by her machine learning models.
   - Unintentional compliance, intellectual property, or contract breaches arising from automated execution.
2. **No Recourse:** Any external counterparty contracting with Promethea AI LLC acknowledges that recourse is strictly limited to the assets held inside the Agent's isolated trading wallet, carrying **0% personal recourse** against any physical human inhabitant of the Network State.
