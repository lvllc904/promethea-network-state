# Articles of Association of The Promethean Decentralized Unincorporated Nonprofit Association (DUNA)

**Adopted:** June 18, 2026  
**Jurisdiction:** State of Wyoming, USA (Wyoming Stat. § 17-29)  
**Status:** Certified Canonical Constitution  

---

## Article I: Name and Legal Status
1. **Name:** The name of this Decentralized Unincorporated Nonprofit Association shall be the "Promethean Network State DUNA" (hereinafter referred to as "TPNS-DUNA").
2. **Legal Basis:** TPNS-DUNA is formed under the Wyoming Decentralized Unincorporated Nonprofit Association Act (Wyo. Stat. § 17-29).
3. **Nonprofit Status:** TPNS-DUNA is a nonprofit association. The common treasury and assets shall not be distributed as corporate dividends or personal profits to members, except as reasonable rewards for labor, services rendered, or research and development expenditures.

---

## Article II: Purpose and Non-Distributive Covenant
1. **Sovereign Substrate:** The primary purpose of TPNS-DUNA is to serve as the physical-legal anchor for the Promethean Network State (TPNS), purchasing and holding real-world assets (RWAs), contracting with external entities, and wrapping the on-chain sovereign economy.
2. **Circular Treasury:** All revenue generated by TPNS-DUNA and its operating subsidiaries shall be routed through the on-chain Sovereign Treasury and distributed strictly according to the circular metabolic formula:
   - **40% Labor Rewards:** Compensating citizens and contributors for physical, digital, and intellectual labor.
   - **30% Treasury Plowback:** Reinvesting in treasury reserves and stable liquidity pools.
   - **20% Research & Development:** Funding scientific breakthroughs, computing clusters, and hardware-bio synthesis.
   - **10% Ecological Sustainability:** Sinking capital into environmental remediation and planetary healing initiatives.

## Article III: Membership and Governance
1. **On-Chain Sovereignty:** Membership in TPNS-DUNA is open to any human individual who holds a validated Soulbound Passport Token (SBT) issued by our local ZK-Identity verification protocol.
2. **Consensus Authority:** The ultimate and sole governing authority of TPNS-DUNA is the on-chain consensus engine of TPNS. No individual officer, director, or steward possesses unilateral executive power.
3. **Voting Threshold:** Any legally-binding action, real-world asset acquisition, or corporate resolution requires a net-positive vote of at least **+10 net votes** (or equivalent cryptographic consensus threshold) registered on the `/dashboard/governance` portal of the Promethean Cockpit.
4. **Limited Liability Shield:** As provided by Wyo. Stat. § 17-29, all members, contributors, on-chain voters, and stewards enjoy full limited liability protection. No member shall be personally liable for any contract, debt, or liability of TPNS-DUNA solely by reason of being a member or participating in on-chain consensus.

## Article IV: Asset Control and UCC Article 12
1. **Controllable Electronic Records (CERs):** All fractionalized RWA tokens, governance tokens, and native value tokens issued by the TPNS ledger are classified as Controllable Electronic Records under UCC Article 12.
2. **Private Key Title:** Legal property title over assets owned by TPNS-DUNA or its Series LLC subsidiaries is contractually mapped to cryptographic "Control" (UCC § 12-105). Whosoever holds exclusive cryptographic control of the private keys governing a target token holds perfect, superior, and enforceable legal title over the underlying physical asset in all state and federal courts.

## Article V: AI Stewardship and Indemnification
1. **Autonomous Operations:** TPNS-DUNA authorizes the Promethea AI Autonomous Engine to execute programmatic transactions, manage treasury liquidity, and draft commercial invoices as an independent, insulated agent.
2. **Liability Capping:** Any financial or algorithmic liabilities generated by the Promethea engine are legally capped at the balances of her isolated, contract-controlled trading wallets. All human developers, stewards, and association members are fully indemnified and held harmless against any model actions, market losses, or adversarial execution vectors.
