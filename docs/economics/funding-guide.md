# Global Strategic Funding Guide (2026) — Contact & Filing Directory

This directory provides the administrative and strategic data required to apply for high-impact funding in AI, DeFi, and Socio-Economic development. It is designed to bridge the gap between visionary technical concepts and the institutional or decentralized capital required to realize them.

## 1. AI, AGI, & Frontier Tech (VR/AR/EV)

### 🟢 Google.org: AI for Government Innovation
*   **Award**: $1M – $3M + Pro bono technical support from Google Fellowships.
*   **Application Link**: [Apply via Submittable](https://www.submittable.com/)
*   **Deadline**: April 3, 2026.
*   **Expanded Context**: This fund focuses on "Agentic AI"—AI that doesn't just answer questions but performs tasks across government silos.
*   **Generic Template Suggestion**:
    *   **Title**: [Project Name]: Agentic AI for [City/Agency Name] [Service Area]
    *   **Executive Summary**: We are a [Social Enterprise/Nonprofit] partnering with [Government Agency] to implement an agentic AI solution that streamlines [Specific Public Service].
    *   **Implementation**: Our project utilizes LLM-based agents to interface with legacy government databases, automating [Process] to reduce human overhead.
*   **Advice**: Focus on "Transformative" use cases. Google wants to see a "seamless front door" where citizens can access all services through a single, intelligent interface.

### 🟢 Meta: AI Glasses Impact Grants
*   **Award**: $25k – $50k (Accelerator) | $200k (Catalyst).
*   **Application Link**: [Meta AI Glasses Grant Portal](https://meta.com/grants)
*   **Deadline**: March 9, 2026 (Annual Cycle).
*   **Expanded Context**: Meta is looking for developers who can prove that "Spatial Computing" has a social utility beyond gaming, specifically in accessibility, education, or field work.
*   **Generic Template Suggestion**:
    *   **Project**: [Project Name] - Wearable AI for [Community Impact Area].
    *   **Innovation**: Utilizing the Meta Wearables Device Access Toolkit to provide [Specific Value, e.g., real-time agricultural data/memory assistance].
    *   **Social Metric**: We aim to improve [Outcome] for [User Group] by removing the friction of handheld devices in the field.
*   **Advice**: They are specifically looking for "Real-time" applications. Mention the "Device Access Toolkit" and "Multimodal Llama 3/4" integration explicitly in your tech stack.

### 🟢 Stanford HAI: Hoffman-Yee Grant
*   **Contact**: hai-grants@lists.stanford.edu
*   **Deadline**: LOI due Jan 28, 2026; Full Proposal due April 1, 2026.
*   **Expanded Context**: This is arguably the most prestigious academic AI grant. It favors "Moonshot" research that standard corporate R&D might find too risky or socially complex.
*   **Generic Template Suggestion**:
    *   **Header**: LOI for Hoffman-Yee Grant: [Interdisciplinary Title]
    *   **Problem**: Current AI in [Sector] lacks [Human-Centered Element], leading to [Harm/Inefficiency].
    *   **Approach**: Combine [Technical AI/AGI Research] with [Social Science/Legal Insight] to create a framework for [Human Agency/Safety].
*   **Strict Rule**: The LOI must be a self-contained PDF. Do not include links; if the reviewer can't read it on a printed page, it doesn't exist.

### 🟢 U.S. DOT: SMART Grants (EV/Mobility)
*   **Contact**: smart@dot.gov | Phone: (202) 774-8003
*   **Expanded Context**: Specifically targets "Smart Grid" integration for EVs and the use of AR/VR in traffic management and urban planning.
*   **Generic Template Suggestion**:
    *   **Project Narrative**: [Project Name] Stage 1 Demonstration.
    *   **Safety/Equity**: Our [EV/VR/AI] solution addresses the safety gap in [Underserved Neighborhood] by [Mechanism].
    *   **Tech Stack**: Utilizing V2G (Vehicle-to-Grid) protocols and [Specific Tech] to stabilize local energy markets.
*   **Readiness**: Must be registered in SAM.gov (UEI required).

## 2. DeFi, DACs, & Autonomous Governance

### 🟢 Arbitrum Foundation Grant Program
*   **Application Link**: [Arbitrum Grant Hub](https://arbitrum.foundation/grants)
*   **Expanded Context**: Arbitrum is currently focused on "Gaming" and "Governance Tooling." They want DACs to be easier to manage and protocols to be stickier for users.
*   **Generic Template Suggestion**:
    *   **Abstract**: [Project Name] is a [Tool/dApp] that increases ARB utility by [Function].
    *   **Technical Rationale**: Leveraging Stylus (WASM) to enable high-performance [Feature] on-chain.
    *   **Milestones**: 1. Stylus-based MVP; 2. Security Audit; 3. Mainnet Launch.
*   **Advice**: Socialize your idea on the Arbitrum Forum first. The community "delegates" hold the power; if they like you, the Foundation is more likely to approve.

### 🟢 Ethereum Foundation: Ecosystem Support (ESP)
*   **Contact**: [Office Hours Inquiry Form](https://esp.ethereum.foundation/)
*   **Expanded Context**: The EF is interested in "The Verge" and "The Purge" (Ethereum roadmap phases). Anything that makes running a node easier or helps with ZK-proof generation is a high priority.
*   **Generic Template Suggestion**:
    *   **Project Summary**: We are building [Open Source Tool] to support [Scaling/Privacy/UX] on Ethereum.
    *   **Public Good**: This tool is 100% open-source and benefits all L2/L1 developers. No token-launch intent.
*   **Advice**: Do not lead with a "Business Plan." Lead with "Technical Contribution to the Protocol."

### 🟢 Gitcoin: Grants Stack (DeSci & Public Goods)
*   **Application Link**: [Gitcoin Explorer](https://explorer.gitcoin.co/)
*   **Cycles**: Multiple rounds (GG24, GG25) throughout 2026.
*   **Expanded Context**: DeSci (Decentralized Science) is a booming category. If you are using blockchain to fund or verify scientific research (AI, Longevity, Physics), this is your primary source.
*   **Generic Template Suggestion**:
    *   **Title**: [Project Name]: Decentralized [Science/Infrastructure] for [Community].
    *   **Value Proposition**: Using Quadratic Funding to solve the "Prisoner's Dilemma" in scientific funding for [Subject].
*   **Advice**: Build a "community shill list" (Twitter/X and Farcaster) early; Gitcoin is a popularity-based matching fund.

## 3. Socio-Economic Betterment & Global Finance

### 🟢 MacArthur Foundation: Humanity AI
*   **Award**: $500k – $2M.
*   **Application Link**: [MacArthur Press/Grants](https://www.macfound.org/)
*   **Focus**: Advancing AI "By and For People," civil society infrastructure, and global dialogues.
*   **Expanded Context**: This is for the "Socio-Economic Betterment" of the global south and marginalized groups. It is less about "Tech" and more about "Justice."
*   **Generic Template Suggestion**:
    *   **Focus**: Deepening civic engagement through [AI Initiative].
    *   **Global Dialogue**: Launching the [Series Name] to address shared AI challenges in [Region/Topic].
*   **Advice**: Frame your project around "Human Rights" and "Global Equity." They want to see how AI prevents the concentration of power.

### 🟢 USAID: Development Innovation Ventures (DIV)
*   **Contact**: div@usaid.gov
*   **Expanded Context**: DIV is a "venture capital" arm of the US government. They fund social startups in emerging markets that can prove their impact through rigorous data (RCTs).
*   **Generic Template Suggestion**:
    *   **Challenge**: [Poverty/Finance Issue] affects [Number] people in [Country].
    *   **Solution**: Our [Innovation] solves this at $[Cost] vs $[Standard Cost].
    *   **Evidence**: We have conducted a pilot showing [X%] improvement in [Financial Inclusion Metric].
*   **Advice**: Focus heavily on "Development impact per dollar." If you can't show a lower cost-of-delivery than traditional aid, you won't get funded.

### 🟢 DFC (U.S. Int. Development Finance Corp)
*   **Application Link**: [DFC Apply Page](https://www.dfc.gov/apply)
*   **Expanded Context**: This is for "Global Finance" and "Emerging Markets." They provide credit guarantees and direct loans to projects that are too risky for commercial banks but too profitable for standard grants.
*   **Generic Template Suggestion**:
    *   **Overview**: [Company Name] seeks $[Amount] in [Loan/Equity] to expand [FinTech/EV/AGI Infra] in [Emerging Market].
    *   **Additionality**: Private local banks have declined due to [Market Risk]; DFC involvement will catalyze $[Amount] in additional private capital.
*   **Advice**: Highlight "Additionality"—why DFC is the only bridge that makes this project viable.

## 5. Strategic Filing Advice & Formalities

### The "Impact-to-Alpha" Ratio
In 2026, funders are weary of "AI hype." To stand out, your application must balance:
*   **Technical Alpha**: What is the technical moat or innovation? (e.g., a new AGI safety protocol).
*   **Social Impact**: How many people does this pull out of poverty or protect from harm?

### The "One-Pager" Framework
1.  **The Hook (10s)**: What is the one sentence that makes them stop scrolling?
2.  **The Proof (20s)**: Why are you the only team that can do this?
3.  **The Ask (30s)**: Exactly how much money, and what specific milestone does it unlock?

*   **Tranche B (40%)**: Pilot Deployment with Government Partner [Name] (Month 4-8).
*   **Tranche C (30%)**: Security Audit & Open Source Documentation (Month 9-12).
